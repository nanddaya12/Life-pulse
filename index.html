How LifePulse Works

A seamless real-time platform engineered specifically for urgent medical blood shortages.

1. Instant Emergency Broadcast

Hospitals or relatives submit an urgent request with required blood units, location, and hospital room details.

2. Real-Time Smart Matching

Our matching algorithm instantly filters verified active donors by exact blood group compatibility and city proximity.

3. Direct Donor Alert

Compatible donors receive live broadcast alerts on their dashboard with instant emergency contact options to respond immediately.
